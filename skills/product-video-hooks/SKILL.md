# Product Video Hooks — First 3 Seconds Engine

Write batches of **4–10 radically different openers** (`0:00–0:03`) for TikTok, Reels, Shorts, and Meta product ads.

See [references/hook-bank.md](references/hook-bank.md) for **60+ proven hook templates** across awareness stages.

---

## 1. The 3-Layer Agreement Rule (`50%+ Hook Rate Formula`)

Every single hook must be delivered as **three simultaneous, agreeing layers**:

| Layer | Specification | Example (Origan + Nigelle Softgels — FR) |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Frame-0 Visual (`0:00`)** | Immediate action, contrast, or human/stickman gesture on frame 1 | Main tenant 2 gélules dorées translucides face à 4 flacons classiques barrés |
| **2. Spoken Line (`6–9 words`)** | Fits inside `2.5–3.2s` at natural speech speed | *"Arrête d'empiler quatre compléments chaque hiver. Regarde ça."* |
| **3. Text Overlay (`≤ 8 words`)** | High-contrast text inside 9:16 safe zone saying the same core promise | **`4 flacons remplacés par 1 seul 👇`** |

**Golden Rule:** *Relevant beats loud.* Never use a fake unrelated stunt hook that drops `85%` of viewers at second 4. Every hook must bridge naturally into the product by second `5–8`.

---

## 2.Vary the Hook TYPE, Never Just the Wording

When asked for *"5 hooks"* or *"10 hooks"*, pick **at least 5 distinct archetypes**:
1. **Callout / Pain Question** (*Problem-Aware*): *"Coup de barre à 15h tous les jours ? Voici pourquoi."*
2. **Hidden Culprit / Mechanism** (*Unaware*): *"La vraie raison pour laquelle l'huile d'origan liquide finit au placard..."*
3. **Skeptical / Confession** (*Product-Aware*): *"Je pensais que l'huile de nigelle était surcotée… puis j'ai vu ces 5 études."*
4. **Late Discovery** (*Unaware*): *"Comment j'ai pu passer 30 ans sans connaître l'association origan + nigelle ?"*
5. **Price / Stack Anchor** (*Solution-Aware*): *"Au lieu d'acheter l'origan et la nigelle séparément, regarde cette formule."*
6. **Payoff / Satisfying Macro First** (*Any*): Macro shot of golden softgels catching morning sunlight + crisp pouch snap.
7. **Reply-to-Comment Card** (*Product-Aware*): Overlay comment bubble *"« C'est quoi la différence entre la nigelle pure et ce complexe ? »"* + direct answer.

## 3. Arena Hook-Swap Workflow for CapCut

To create **3 Hook Variants of an existing project** in `< 2 minutes`:
1. Generate 3 distinct `scene_01_hookA.png`, `scene_01_hookB.png`, `scene_01_hookC.png` (each with a completely different Frame-0 composition so Meta Andromeda treats them as separate creatives).
2. Generate the 3 matching `scene_01_hookX.mp3` voiceovers via `generate_speech`.
3. Clone or swap Scene 1 in `manifest.json` while keeping Scenes `02..N` untouched!
