# TikTok Shop & Affiliate Video Playbook (2025–2026 Compliance)

TikTok Shop strictly enforces anti-spam rules that differ sharply from Meta Ads and standard organic reels. **Read this skill whenever the video carries a TikTok Shop product link (orange cart / panier orange).**

---

## 1. TikTok Shop Red-Line Rules (Account Protection)

| Allowed & Rewarded on TikTok Shop | Penalized / Violation Risk on TikTok Shop |
|---|---|
| **Real human voice & real person visible** (face or hands-on real footage `≥ 3s`) | **AI TTS voice acting as the presenter** or 100% still-image slideshow (triggers Shop demotion / link removal) |
| **Product visible from `0:00–0:02`** + hook directly tied to the product | Unrelated clickbait hooks or screenshots of product pages |
| **Exact price/deal ONLY if live right now** on the TikTok Shop page | Saying *"Moins de 15€"* or *"Vente flash"* if the live price changed (causes retroactive violation!) — **safer: say *"Vérifie l'offre sur le panier orange en bas"*** |
| **1 specific factual number** (*"60 softgels = 30 jours"*, *"Sans OGM"*) | Medical cure claims, weight-loss promises, or body before/after |
| **AI B-Roll labeled AIGC** cut *under* a real human creator's shoot | 5+ near-duplicate low-effort reposts within 7 days (hides Shop links) |

---

## 2. Top-Converting 25–30s TikTok Shop Script Blueprint

```text
0:00–0:03  HOOK   Real product in hand at Frame 0 + direct problem/curiosity callout
                  Overlay (≤ 7 words, safe zone): same promise as spoken line
0:03–0:13  DEMO   Show the pouch + open/hold softgels in real room light + 1 concrete spec
                  (Optional: cut in 1–2 crisp Arena macro/infographic B-roll inserts)
0:13–0:22  PROOF  Why this format beats alternatives (no burning liquid taste, 2-in-1 formula,
                  real buyer feedback read honestly)
0:22–0:28  CTA    "J'ai épinglé le sachet juste en bas dans le panier orange — clique pour voir
                  s'il en reste en stock." (No hard-coded price that could expire!)
```

## 3. Hybrid Arena + Phone Workflow for TikTok Shop

When the user wants a **TikTok Shop** video:
1. Deliver the **word-for-word French/English script + 5-shot iPhone filming checklist** (`3 minutes` to record on phone: chest-height hook, label close-up, softgel in palm, kitchen counter shot, honest face-cam CTA).
2. Generate **high-impact visual B-roll & proof overlays in Arena** (`overlays/product_pouch.png`, ingredient visuals, study/comparison cards) and pre-place them in a CapCut Windows draft so the user simply drops their 15s phone recording onto Track 1 in CapCut!
