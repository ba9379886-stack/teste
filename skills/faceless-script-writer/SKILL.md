# Faceless Storytelling & Documentary Script Writer

Write captivating faceless voiceover scripts where **every single sentence earns the next 5 seconds of retention** — equally deadly for `50s` vertical Shorts/TikToks (like *Point Nemo*) and `6–10 min` YouTube documentaries.

---

## 1. Topic Qualification Test (Must Pass At Least One)

Before writing, ensure the angle hits at least one viral curiosity trigger:
- **Superlative + Concrete Number:** *"À 2 688 kilomètres de toute terre habitée..."* (measurable extreme).
- **Knowledge / Irony Gap:** *"Les humains les plus proches de cet endroit ne sont même pas sur Terre — ils passent à 400 km au-dessus, dans l'ISS."*
- **Counterintuitive Premise:** *"Pourquoi la graine la plus étudiée du Moyen-Orient reste absente de 90 % des pharmacies modernes."*
- **Escalating Mystery / Case Loop:** Every answer opens a deeper anomaly.

**Fact Integrity Rule:** Verify dates, distances, clinical study counts, and historical names via `web_search` before writing. Never invent statistics or soften guesses with *"paraît-il"*.

---

## 2. Hook Engine (Cold Drop + Stakes — Zero Throat-Clearing)

- **For Short-Form (`45–60s` Vertical 9:16):**
  - `0:00–0:03` (**Cold Drop**): Drop straight onto the sharpest visual image or number.
  - `0:03–0:09` (**Stakes / Twist**): State the eerie or counterintuitive truth that makes scrolling away impossible.
- **For Long-Form (`5–10 min` YouTube):**
  - `0:00–0:10` (**Cold Drop**), `0:10–0:30` (**Stakes Sentence**), `0:30–0:55` (**3-Peak Trailer** teasing mid-video turns without spoiling the answer, then hard-cut to exact date & place).
- **Strictly Banned in Hooks:** *"Bonjour à tous"*, *"Dans cette vidéo nous allons découvrir"*, *"N'oublie pas de t'abonner avant qu'on commence"*.

---

## 3. Sentence-Level Retention Engineering & Anti-AI-Slop Laws

1. **Narrate in the Present Tense:** History, science, and geography feel immediate when told in the present (*"À 4 000 mètres de profondeur, la lumière disparaît..."*).
2. **Concrete Number Every `15–30s`:** Replace vague adjectives (*"très loin"*, *"beaucoup d'études"*) with exact figures (*"2 688 kilomètres"*, *"94 patients sur 12 semaines"*).
3. **Open Loops Between Scenes:** Never close a question in the same breath you open it; close the previous loop while planting the next one.
4. **Vary Sentence Cadence:** Follow a `16–20 word` atmospheric sentence with a `4–6 word` punch.
5. **Zero AI Slop:** Ban mirrored chatbot cadences (*"Ce n'est pas juste X. C'est Y."*), philosophical filler, and vague transitions (*"Il est intéressant de noter que..."*).

---

## 4. Arena Execution for Faceless Videos

Pair directly with Arena's native studio:
- **Voice:** Deep, calm, or intriguing `fr-FR` narration via `generate_speech` (`voice_id="voice-00"` or newly auditioned voice).
- **Visuals:** Cohesive visual palette across all `7–9 scenes` (`generate_image` in `9:16`), alternating wide scale shots, maps/diagrams, and intimate close-ups every `5–6 seconds`.
- **CapCut Polish:** Ken Burns camera motion (`zoom_in`, `zoom_out`, `pan_up`), atmospheric transitions (`Fondu au noir`, `Fondu`), subtle `12%` ambient BGM, and crisp `3–6 word` subtitles.
