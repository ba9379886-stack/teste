# Video Marketing Strategy & Platform Optimization

Plan high-retention video marketing systems across short-form (`9:16`) and long-form (`16:9`) channels, matching each platform's algorithmic retention gates and viewer psychology.

---

## 1. Platform Hook Windows & Optimal Durations

**71% of viewers decide whether to keep watching within the first 3 seconds.** Design the opening frame and pacing around each platform's exact scroll behavior:

| Platform | Hook Window | Optimal Length | Primary Algorithm Signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TikTok (Organic & Ads)** | **`0–2s`** (value clear by `3s`, hook complete by `6s`) | `21–34s` (fast product ads) / `45–60s` (story & proof explainers) | Completion rate, rewatches, shares, saves |
| **Instagram Reels** | **`0–3s`** | `30–60s` (`9:16`, aesthetic clarity + punchy captions) | Watch time %, **DM shares**, and **saves** |
| **YouTube Shorts** | **`0–3.5s`** | `45–58s` (strong narrative payoff & loop ending) | Viewed vs. Swiped-Away % (aim `> 72%` viewed) |
| **Meta Paid Reels / Feed** | **`0–3s`** (brand/product context by `5s`) | `20–45s` (`9:16` + `4:5` feed cut) | 3s Hook Rate (`> 28%`), 15s Hold Rate (`> 12%`), CPA / ROAS |
| **YouTube Long-Form** | **`0–15s`** cold drop + stakes (`0–60s` full intro) | `6–12 min` | Click-Through Rate (`> 6%`) + Average View Duration (`> 45%`) |

## 2. Sound-Off & Safe-Zone Engineering (9:16 Vertical `1080x1920`)

Up to **85% of feed impressions start on mute** (especially Meta/Instagram). Every video built in our CapCut pipeline must pass the **Mute Test**:
1. **Synchronized Burned-In Subtitles:** Short chunks (`3–6 words` per line, bottom-center above the platform caption bar, `y ≈ -0.62` in CapCut coordinates).
2. **Visual Proof Overlays (`overlays/`):** Show the actual product pouch (`product_pouch.png`) and visual proof cards (PubMed study headers, comparison badges) so a viewer on mute still understands: *(a) what the product/topic is, (b) why it's credible, and (c) what to do next*.
3. **9:16 UI Safe Zones (`1080x1920`):**
   - **Top `0–250 px`:** Avoid (search bar / profile header).
   - **Bottom `1450–1920 px`:** Avoid critical graphics (platform username, caption, shop button).
   - **Right edge `920–1080 px` (lower half):** Avoid (like/comment/bookmark/share icons).
   - **Sweet spot for study headers & product callouts:** Upper-middle (`y = +0.55` to `+0.65` in CapCut) and center canvas (`y = -0.20` to `+0.35`).

## 3. Weekly Content Pillar Mix (Brand & Creator Growth)

To grow a brand or faceless channel without ad fatigue, rotate 4 pillars rather than repeating pure sales pitches:
- **40% Educational / Curiosity Authority** (e.g., *5 études scientifiques sur la nigelle*, *Pourquoi personne ne connaît ce mécanisme*) — drives **Saves & Shares**.
- **25% Problem-Aware Relatable / POV / Stick Man** (daily friction, myths debunked, fake vs. real solutions) — drives **Comments & Reach**.
- **20% Product Demo / Proof / Social Proof** (ingredients, manufacturing quality, real customer feedback) — builds **Trust**.
- **15% Direct Offer / Conversion CTA** (bundle, seasonal urgency, clear link-in-bio / shop push) — harvests **Sales**.

## 4. Diagnostic Playbook When a Video Underperforms

- **Low 3s Hook Rate (`< 22%`) / High Swipe-Away:** Keep the body, rewrite **Frame 0 visual + first spoken sentence** (test 3–5 radically different openers).
- **Good Hook (`> 30%`) but Drop-Off at `5–12s`:** Missing **Second Hook** or slow throat-clearing after the intro — cut filler and drop straight into point #1 or conflict.
- **High Watch Time, Low Clicks / Sales:** Product appeared too late (show product by `3–8s` and again at CTA) or CTA asked for 3 actions instead of **one single action** (*"Clique sur le lien en bio"*).
