## 1. Angle First, Hook Second (`Buyer × Problem × Promise`)

Never write hooks before locking the **Angle**:
- **Unaware:** Surprising truth, study breakdown, or curiosity story (*"Pourquoi les médecins s'intéressent de près à cette graine noire utilisée depuis 2000 ans..."*).
- **Problem-Aware:** Name the exact daily friction + why standard solutions disappoint (*"Fatigue hivernale à répétition : pourquoi la vitamine C seule ne suffit souvent pas..."*).
- **Solution-Aware:** **Us vs. Them** on ONE decisive mechanism (*"Huile d'origan + Huile de nigelle réunies dans une seule capsule softgel sans arrière-goût brûlant"*).
- **Product / Most-Aware:** Social proof count + bundle/offer + urgency CTA.

## 2. Meta + Toluna Reels Conversion Multipliers (Apply to Every Build)

Research across high-spending vertical e-commerce ads shows:
- **Listing concrete benefits clearly:** `5.3×` lift
- **Product shown more than once** (early `2–8s` + Proof/CTA `last 8s`): `2.7×` lift
- **Speech + subtle background music (`~12%` volume):** `2.1×` lift
- **Explicit spoken + on-screen CTA:** `1.9×` lift
- **On-screen text ceiling:** `5–8 words` per card so viewers on mute never fall behind.

## 3. Creative Testing Batch Plan (How Brands Find Winners)

Only `5–8%` of cold ads become scalable winners. Meta's Andromeda engine groups visually similar ads together — so **a real test batch varies the CONCEPT and FIRST FRAME, not just caption colors**:
- **Concept 1:** Scientific / Study Authority (e.g., *Stick Man + 5 PubMed Studies + Product Reveal*)
- **Concept 2:** Phone-Real AI Presenter / Lifestyle Kitchen Routine (`ai-ugc-ad-video`)
- **Concept 3:** Macro Ingredient Synergy & Us-vs-Them Comparison (`ugc-video-ads`)
- Once one concept wins on CPA/ROAS, generate **4–5 new 3-second Hook Scenes** (`product-video-hooks`) on top of the winning body!
