# UGC Creator Campaign Brief, Claim Audit & Compliance Skill

Protect ad accounts, brands, and creators from ad rejections, TikTok Shop penalties, FTC fines, and French **DGCCRF / Loi Influenceurs (Loi n° 2023-451)** violations while keeping UGC natural and persuasive.

## 1. Line-by-Line Claim Tagging Protocol

Before finalizing any UGC or product ad script, mentally or explicitly audit every sentence under 4 categories:

| Tag | Meaning | Verification Rule |
|---|---|---|
| `[CLAIM: EXPERIENCE]` | First-person lived use (*"Je prends 2 capsules chaque matin..."*) | **Allowed ONLY when spoken/filmed by a real human who actually used the product.** Strictly forbidden for AI presenters/voices. |
| `[CLAIM: BRAND / SCIENCE]` | Objective product spec or published research (*"60 softgels sans OGM"*, *"Étude PubMed 2010 sur 94 patients"*) | Must match the real packaging / product page / verified PubMed abstract word-for-word. |
| `[CLAIM: NONE]` | Narrative bridge, question, or visual cue | Check that no hidden superlative (*"#1"*, *"guérit"*, *"100% garanti"*) slipped in untagged. |
| `[CLAIM: CUT]` | Exaggerated or unprovable claim from a raw brief | Rewrite into a compliant `[CLAIM: BRAND / SCIENCE]` statement or cut completely. |

## 2. French (Loi Influenceurs / DGCCRF / EFSA) & US (FTC) Red Lines

1. **Mandatory Commercial Intent Clarity (France Loi du 9 juin 2023 & ARPP):**
   - Sponsored/affiliate creator posts in France must display **`Collaboration commerciale`** or **`Publicité`** clearly and legibly (not hidden behind *"voir plus"* or drowned in hashtags; never say *"Ceci n'est pas une pub"*).
   - If an image or video uses AI-generated faces/silhouettes in commercial influencer content in France or on TikTok, enable platform **AI-generated content (`Contenu généré par l'IA` / `AIGC`)** labels.
2. **Supplements & Natural Health Products (EFSA / DGCCRF / Meta):**
   - **Forbidden:** Claiming to prevent, treat, or cure human disease (*"soigne le diabète"*, *"guérit l'hypertension"*, *"élimine les virus"*).
   - **Compliant & High-Impact:** Cite real peer-reviewed clinical literature accurately (*"Étudié cliniquement sur..."*, *"Riche en thymoquinone et carvacrol"*, *"Soutient les défenses naturelles / contribue au métabolisme normal"*).
3. **Make-Money / Business Claims:**
   - Ban get-rich-quick framing (*"revenu passif garanti"*, *"quitte ton job en 7 jours"*). Any revenue figure requires documented typical results.

## 3. Deliverable When Reviewing a Brief or Preparing a Human Shoot

1. **Brief Audit Table:** What is safe to say, what needs rewording, and any missing product facts.
2. **Claim-Tagged Script:** Spoken lines + `[CLAIM: ...]` tags + exact source/proof notes.
3. **5-Shot Smartphone Filming Checklist** (when a real creator films on iPhone) + **9:16 Safe-Zone Check** (keep text clear of top `14%`, bottom `35%`, and right `15%` action icons).
